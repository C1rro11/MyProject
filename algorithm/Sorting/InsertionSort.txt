Insertion is performed by successively
swapping 𝐴[𝑖] with items to its left until an
item smaller than 𝐴[𝑖] is found.

Implementation:
put array A;
for (int i = 2 to n);          
j = i-1
while(A[j] > A[j+1] && j >= 1):
swap(A[j+1],A[j])
j--;
