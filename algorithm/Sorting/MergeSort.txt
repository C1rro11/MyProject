MergeSort
purpose: create 2 sorted lists into a sorted whole list.

implementation:
create 2 new arrays L and R.
L:[p...q] R:[q+1...r]

append inf at the end of L and R.
int i ,j = 1;

for k = p to r.
    if(L[i] <= R[j])
        A[k] = L[i];
        i = i + 1;
    else
        A[k] = R[i];
        j = j + 1;
