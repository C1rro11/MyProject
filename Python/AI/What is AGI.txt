Artificial general intelligence(AGI) is a hypothetical type of AI that can learn,
understand, apply cognitive skills across any intelligence task at human level.

narrow AI(today) vs AGI.
Today’s AI (Narrow): Excels at highly specific tasks (e.g., generating text, playing chess, or identifying diseases) 
but cannot easily transfer that knowledge to unrelated problems.
It requires training for every new use case.

AGI (General): Would act like a human brain. 
It could seamlessly connect concepts across different disciplines, learn new skills from scratch, 
and apply common sense to solve unfamiliar problems.
